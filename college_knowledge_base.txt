4. ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY & ENTRANCE EXAMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A) B.Tech Programs:
   - Eligibility: 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry/Computer Science/Biology with minimum 60% aggregate marks (55% for reserved categories).
   - Accepted Entrance Exams: JEE Main, State CET (Common Entrance Test), or AITS-EAT (Apex Institute Online Entrance Assessment).
   - Management / NRI Quota: 15% of total seats reserved based on 10+2 merit score.

B) BCA / BBA / B.Sc Programs:
   - Eligibility: 10+2 in any stream (Mathematics preferred for BCA/B.Sc) with minimum 50% marks.
   - Selection: Merit list + Personal Interview.

C) M.Tech Programs:
   - Eligibility: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant discipline with minimum 60% or 6.5 CGPA.
   - Accepted Exams: GATE (valid score) or University PG Entrance Test. GATE qualified candidates receive monthly stipend as per AICTE norms.

D) MBA / MCA Programs:
   - MBA Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks. Accepted exams: CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, or State MBA CET.
   - MCA Eligibility: BCA / B.Sc (CS/IT) or Bachelor's with Mathematics at 10+2 or degree level with minimum 50% marks.
